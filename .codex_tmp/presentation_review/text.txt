Conclusion & Perspectives
                        Conclusion




In conclusion, INWIN offers an innovative solution that combines
event management and corporate gift customization within a
single mobile application. It simplifies organization, improves
communication, and provides a better user experience for both
individuals and businesses. As future work, we plan to integrate
online payments, intelligent recommendations, additional
partnerships, and expand the platform to reach a wider market
