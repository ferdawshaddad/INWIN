General Introduction
In today’s digital era, event management has become an essential service for both
individuals and businesses. The increasing need for efficient organization, personalized
services, and smooth communication has led to the emergence of digital platforms that
simplify the planning process and improve user experience.
Titled INWIN, this project aims to provide a comprehensive platform dedicated to
facilitating event organization for both individual clients and businesses. The application
allows users to request event organization services and communicate directly with service
providers, ensuring a smooth, efficient, and transparent planning process.
In addition to event management services, INWIN offers advanced features designed
for businesses. These include the customization and ordering of corporate gifts, with op-
tions such as logo integration, personalized text, and color selection. This functionality
enables companies to strengthen their brand identity and enhance professional relation-
ships through tailored and meaningful gifting solutions.
This report presents a comprehensive study of the development of the INWIN ap-
plication, from its initial conception to its implementation. It begins by introducing the
project context and objectives, followed by an analysis of user needs and market chal-
lenges. It then details the functional and technical specifications of the system, as well
as its architecture and design choices. Finally, the report highlights the application’s
interface and workflow, demonstrating how usability, efficiency, and personalization are
combined within a unified platform.
Ultimately, this project reflects the integration of digital innovation into event man-
agement, offering a practical and user-centered solution that simplifies organizational
processes while meeting the evolving needs of both individuals and businesses.

1.2 Project Presentation
1.2.1 Consumer Behavior
Consumer behavior in the field of event organization has evolved significantly in
recent years. Both individuals and businesses are increasingly looking for simple, fast,
and personalized solutions to organize their events, whether personal or professional.
Instead of managing complex processes involving multiple service providers separately,
users now prefer centralized platforms that offer better visibility and save time.
Usersalsoplacegreatimportanceondirectcommunicationandservicetransparency.
They expect to easily interact with service providers, track their requests, and ensure that
the offered services meet their expectations. Personalization has become a key factor,
especially in choosing event services, themes, and organizational details.
For businesses, these expectations are even more critical. In addition to organizing
professional events such as seminars and conferences, companies seek to strengthen their
brand image through complementary elements like customized corporate gifts. Therefore,

1.2.2 Problem Statement
Event organization, whether personal or professional, requires significant effort, co-
ordination, and time. Many clients struggle to find reliable service providers, manage
communication, and ensure that all event elements are aligned with their expectations.
For businesses, the challenge is even more complex, as they must not only organize
professional events but also manage corporate gifts. These gifts often require customiza-
tion, such as logo integration, personalized text, and color selection. However, this process
is usually handled separately from event planning, leading to inefficiencies, lack of coor-
dination, and limited personalization.
Without a centralized digital solution, these processes remain manual, fragmented,
and difficult to manage, which negatively impacts both efficiency and user experience.
This leads to the following question: How can we design a digital platform that
simplifies event organization, centralizes communication, and integrates corporate gift
management with personalized customization for both individuals and businesses?
1.2.3 Project Idea
The idea behind the INWIN project emerged from the lack of comprehensive digital
solutions that effectively support event organization while integrating complementary
services such as corporate gifting.
INWIN is designed as a centralized digital platform that enables users to plan and
managetheireventsinasimpleandefficientway. Theapplicationallowsuserstosearchfor
service providers, customize services according to their needs, and communicate directly
with providers to ensure better coordination.
For businesses, INWIN also offers a dedicated corporate gift management feature,
including customization options such as logo integration, color selection, and personalized

Targeting is a strategic process that consists of selecting one or more market seg-

3.3.4 App Security
Application Security is one of the core components of the INWIN framework, since
the framework manages users’ accounts, customers’ queries, communications, and docu-
ments shared between the parties. This sub-section describes the key application security
approaches used for securing user data, managing system feature access, and ensuring the
integrity of data storage.
3.3.4.1 Authentication
Authentication basically involves ensuring the identity of the user accessing the
application. In the case of the INWIN application, authentication was carried out through
Firebase Authentication. Once logged in, Firebase authenticates the user and links him
to his respective record in Cloud Firestore, ensuring that each authenticated user has
a user record within the Firestore database, and the sessions stay consistent with other
application data.
Authentication is achieved through logic that determines whether the authenticated
user already has a record in the users collection. If there is no record, a default record
will be created automatically, guaranteeing that each user has at least a record in the
database. Moreover, the application listens continually to the changes in authentication
status, thus being able to authenticate each user instantly upon logging in and binding
him to the correct Firestore record.
